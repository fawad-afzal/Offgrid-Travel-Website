Before you travel

Travel smarter

A little preparation can make your journey more comfortable and enjoyable.

  • Check passport and visa requirements.
  • Review weather and seasonal conditions.
  • Choose suitable clothing and footwear.
  • Keep important documents secure.
  • Respect local traditions and the environment.

Here is an example of how a week-long adventure could be organised. Activities depend on the destination, weather, and local conditions.

A little preparation goes a long way

Three ways to travel better

01

Pack with purpose

Carry what you need, check the weather, and leave room for souvenirs and unexpected discoveries.

02

Connect with culture

Learn a few local phrases, respect customs, and support local businesses wherever possible.

03

Leave time to explore

Do not overfill your schedule. Some of the best travel moments happen when you slow down.
